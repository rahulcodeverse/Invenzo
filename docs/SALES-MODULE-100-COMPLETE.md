## 📊 Customer Payments Module Details

**File**: `payment-form.component.ts` (~310 lines, inline template)

### Features Implemented ✅:

**Payment Recording**:
- ✅ Customer selector (searchable)
- ✅ Payment date picker
- ✅ Payment amount input
- ✅ **5 Payment Methods**:
  - Cash
  - Bank Transfer
  - Cheque
  - Credit Card
  - UPI
- ✅ Reference number field (transaction ID, cheque no, etc.)
- ✅ Notes field (optional)

**Invoice Allocation**:
- ✅ **Auto-loads outstanding invoices** for selected customer
- ✅ Shows invoice details:
  - Invoice number
  - Invoice date
  - Due date
  - Total amount
  - Balance due
- ✅ **Checkbox selection** per invoice
- ✅ **Allocate amount** input per invoice
- ✅ **Auto-fill logic**:
  - When invoice selected, auto-fills balance or remaining payment
  - Prevents over-allocation
- ✅ **Real-time validation**:
  - Cannot allocate more than invoice balance
  - Cannot allocate more than payment amount

**Smart Calculations**:
- ✅ **Payment Amount**: Total being paid
- ✅ **Total Allocated**: Sum of all allocations
- ✅ **Unallocated**: Payment - Allocated
- ✅ **Visual indicators**:
  - Green when fully allocated
  - Orange when partial
  - Red when unallocated amount

**Integration**:
- ✅ **Quick Payment from Invoice**:
  - Click "Pay" button in invoice list
  - Auto-navigates to payment form
  - Pre-selects customer
  - Pre-fills amount with invoice balance
- ✅ **Success Notification**
- ✅ **Auto-redirect** to invoice list

**Validation**:
- ✅ Required: Customer, Date, Amount, Payment Method
- ✅ At least one invoice must be allocated
- ✅ Allocation cannot exceed invoice balance
- ✅ Submit disabled until valid
